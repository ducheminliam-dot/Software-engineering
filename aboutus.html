About Us

Our Story

A Taste of Italy in the Heart of Kokkola

Founded in 2012 on Talonpojankatu, La Piazza was born from a simple passion: bringing authentic, homemade Italian cuisine to Kokkola. Inspired by traditional family trattorias, our founders set out to create a warm gathering place where friends and families can share freshly prepared meals made from time-honored recipes.

Over the years, we have stayed true to our roots—hand-stretching our pizza dough daily, simmering rich tomato sauces for hours, and pairing classic Italian flavors with fresh, locally sourced Finnish ingredients.

Our Philosophy

Fresh Ingredients & Warm Hospitality

We believe great food starts with honest ingredients. From imported Italian olive oils, aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, and San Marzano tomatoes to fresh local produce and Baltic seafood, every dish is prepared to order in our kitchen.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick weekday lunch or celebrating a milestone dinner, our team is dedicated to making you feel right at home.

Our Services

Dine-In Experience

Enjoy a cozy, welcoming atmosphere in our dining room—ideal for romantic dinners, family gatherings, and casual lunches.

Takeaway & Delivery

Craving La Piazza at home? All our pizzas, pastas, and mains are available for quick pickup or local delivery in Kokkola.

Private Events & Catering

Hosting a birthday, corporate lunch, or special celebration? We offer tailored group menus and full catering services.

Dietary Options

Everyone deserves great Italian food. We gladly accommodate vegetarian, vegan, lactose-free, and gluten-free requests.
